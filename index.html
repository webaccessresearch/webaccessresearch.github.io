AccessibilityResearchBot

This page explains the automated visitor that identifies itself as AccessibilityResearchBot/1.0 (+https://webaccessresearch.github.io).

What it does

We study whether people who use screen readers and keyboards can complete common tasks on consumer websites, such as finding a product and choosing an option. The bot reads public pages to decide which sites to study and to note basic public facts, such as whether a site has an accessibility statement.

How it behaves on your site

  • It reads only public pages. It never signs in, creates accounts, places orders, or enters personal or payment information.
  • It follows robots.txt, including Crawl-delay (up to 60 seconds between requests).
  • It makes one request at a time to a site, at least 4 seconds apart, and visits at most 4 sites at once.
  • It never tries to get around CAPTCHAs, bot challenges, rate limits or other blocks. If a site blocks it, it stops and records that the site was blocked.
  • It does not use rotating proxies. It connects from a fixed address.

Browser-based checks

Some checks use the Chromium web browser (the open-source base of Google Chrome), operated by keyboard the way a person using assistive technology would. Those visits send the browser's own, unmodified identity and follow the same rules: public pages only, no sign-in, no personal or payment information, and they stop when blocked.

How to opt out

Add this to your robots.txt:

User-agent: AccessibilityResearchBot
Disallow: /
